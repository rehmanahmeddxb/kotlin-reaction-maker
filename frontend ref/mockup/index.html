help_outline Save vs. ExportWhy Studio has two different options
save1. SAVE DRAFT (Project)
  • Saves your editing session: timeline, layers, PiP coordinates, sticker text, audio levels.
  • Instant saving inside the app database.
  • Allows you to close the app and resume editing later.
  • Not a playable video file.
movie2. EXPORT MP4 (Final Video)
  • Renders and encodes everything into a real .MP4 video file on your device storage.
  • Saved directly to your Movies / Gallery album.
  • Ready to upload to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or WhatsApp.
  • Cannot be re-edited layer-by-layer once rendered.
